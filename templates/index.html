Show me total revenue by department for last quarter
PostgreSQL ▾
Generated SQL

  department,
  SUM(revenue) AS total_revenue
FROM sales
WHERE date >= '2024-01-01'
  AND date < '2024-04-01'
GROUP BY department
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;

What SQL dialects does QueryMind support?

QueryMind natively supports PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, SQL Server, and Google BigQuery with specialized dialect prompt models.

Is my database data private and secure?

Yes. QueryMind executes queries locally or directly on your connected database. Your credentials and passwords are encrypted, and your database rows never train AI models.

Absolutely. QueryMind comes with a built-in 5-table e-commerce demo store and a 3,900-record customer shopping dataset that loads with one click.
