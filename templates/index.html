FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What database engines are supported?

QueryMind natively supports PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, SQL Server, and Google BigQuery with specialized dialect prompt models and drivers.

Is my database data private and secure?

Yes. QueryMind executes queries locally or directly on your connected database. Your credentials and passwords are encrypted, and your database rows never train AI models.

Can I use QueryMind without an existing database?

Absolutely. QueryMind comes with a built-in 5-table e-commerce demo store and a 3,900-record customer shopping dataset that loads with one click.

How does query optimization work?

QueryMind's AI models analyze the AST and query semantics to suggest index improvements, rewrite redundant joins, and avoid table scans.
